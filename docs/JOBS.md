# Jobs and Position System

The job domain is now deep enough to support position-based professions rather than a single generic job label.

A job contains:
- department
- description
- multiple positions

A position contains:
- level
- salary
- education requirement
- stat requirement
- unique progression identity

Employment stores the character's current job, exact position and experience.

Example content is intentionally small during foundation work. The later content phase will populate the full profession trees, positions, requirements, benefits, progression and activities across the world.
